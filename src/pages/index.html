FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Which Blender and Figma versions work?

Figment Bridge is built for Blender 4.0 to 5.x and runs as a plugin in the Figma desktop app. So far it has been tested on Blender 5.0 only. Blender 4.0 to 4.4 are targeted but haven't been tested yet. Testing inside Figma itself is still limited, and the plugin is currently installed from its manifest (Plugins, Development, Import plugin from manifest) rather than from the Figma Community.

Does it run on Windows, macOS and Linux?

It's been tested on Windows. macOS and Linux haven't been tested yet, so we can't promise they work.

Does my design leave my computer?

No. The Figma plugin talks to Blender over a local connection on your own machine (127.0.0.1). Your designs, fonts and images are never uploaded anywhere.

What counts as an import?

Each time you push a selection into Blender, that's one import.

What happens after the {{config.tiers.free.importLimit}} free imports?

Everything you've already imported stays in your Blender files. You can open it, animate it and render it as before. You just can't create new imports until you pick up Regular or Premium.

What's the difference between re-pushing and Live Sync?

On every plan, you can push again after a change and Figment Bridge updates the existing scene in place, keeping your keyframes, modifiers and material tweaks. Live Sync (Premium) does that push for you automatically as you edit in Figma.

Can I upgrade from Regular to Premium?

Yes, you can upgrade later.

Can I get a refund?

Our refund terms are on the refund policy page. If something doesn't work for you, tell us first.

Can I use my licence on more than one machine?

We're finalising how many machines a licence covers. Ask us if you need to know before buying.

What doesn't it support yet?

A few things are not reproduced in Blender yet:

  • Blend modes and layer blur.
  • Shadows on shapes other than rectangles, ellipses and frames.
  • Clipping on rotated frames (axis-aligned frames are fine).
  • Strikethrough text.
